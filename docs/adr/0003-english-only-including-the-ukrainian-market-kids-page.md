# English throughout, including the page for a Ukrainian-market book series

Every page is in English, `/kids` included — even though the children's series is written for the
Ukrainian market, its titles are Ukrainian, and its source essays were published in Ukrainian.

This was chosen with the trade-off named explicitly: it speaks past the Ukrainian parents and teachers
who are the books' actual buyers, in exchange for one voice, one set of copy to maintain, and no i18n
routing. Original titles remain in Cyrillic with English translation alongside, so the font stack must
retain Cyrillic coverage (EB Garamond does).

Recorded because a future reader will see a Ukrainian children's series described in English and assume
it is an oversight. It is not. Reversing it means retrofitting i18n routing across every page, so it
should be reversed deliberately or not at all.
