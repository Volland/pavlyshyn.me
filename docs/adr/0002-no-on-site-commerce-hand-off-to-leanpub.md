# No on-site commerce — every purchase hands off to Leanpub

The site's stated purpose is to sell books, and it deliberately contains no checkout. Every title links
out to its Leanpub page, which owns pricing, variable pricing, coupons, payment, delivery and refunds.

The decisive factor is jurisdictional rather than technical. Volodymyr is resident in Berlin; taking
payment for digital goods directly would pull in EU VAT/OSS registration and handling across member
states, refund obligations, and a materially larger compliance surface — to duplicate machinery Leanpub
already runs well. The cost is margin per sale and not owning the customer relationship.

If direct sales are ever revisited, the trigger should be volume high enough to justify the VAT
apparatus, not dissatisfaction with the hand-off.
