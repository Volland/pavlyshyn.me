# Two Doors — books and services carry equal weight

The homepage presents the book Catalogue and the Service Offers as peers rather than funnelling to one.
This runs against the conventional advice, which was put to Volodymyr directly and declined: a single
primary conversion usually outperforms a choice, and visitors made to choose often choose neither.

It is recorded so nobody later "fixes" the homepage by demoting one door on the assumption that the
split was an oversight. It was the explicit first decision of the project.

The mitigation is that the two doors are visually and verbally distinct — a reader path and a hire path,
not two variations of the same button — rather than a blended pitch that serves neither.

`/projects` was added later and is deliberately *not* a third door. It sits in the nav as a peer page,
but on the homepage it appears as evidence beneath the hire door — shipped tools being a stronger
credential than any bullet on `/services`. The Two Doors remain two.
