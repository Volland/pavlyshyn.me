# No photograph or screenshot appears in its native form

Every raster image on the site is either a public-domain engraving (a Plate) or is put through a duotone
and hatch treatment until it reads as one. Volodymyr's photograph becomes an engraved Frontispiece. Book
covers get a common engraved frame. Product screenshots do not appear at all — each tool on `/projects`
is illustrated by a hand-authored Diagram Plate showing its actual output instead.

The reason is that the design has a single governing metaphor — a printed page — and one untreated
full-colour asset is enough to break it. VS Code screenshots are the worst offenders: dark chrome and
blue accents against aged paper and burnt orange.

The cost is real and worth stating: asking someone to install a VS Code extension without showing them
the product is a genuine conversion penalty, accepted here in exchange for coherence. Each tool's own
docs site carries real screenshots, one click away.

Recorded because adding a screenshot will look like an obvious improvement to whoever touches
`/projects` next, and because the absence of one is a decision rather than an oversight.
